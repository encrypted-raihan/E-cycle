why it matters

Your drawer of "someday" gadgets is part of a global problem.

Every year, the world throws away electronics containing gold, copper, and rare-earth metals worth more than the GDP of most countries — while toxic components leach into soil and water.

E-Cycle exists to interrupt that cycle. When a device is reused instead of dumped, we avoid the mining, manufacturing, and shipping emissions needed to build a replacement — and we keep hazardous e-waste out of landfills entirely.

  • Reuse extends a device's life by an average of 3–5 years
  • Recycled materials cut manufacturing emissions by up to 80%
  • Every rehomed item is one less item burned or buried
